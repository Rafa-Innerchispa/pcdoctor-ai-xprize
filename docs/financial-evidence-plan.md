# Financial evidence plan

## Monthly reporting

May 2026 and June 2026 are recorded as USD 0 FieldSpark revenue unless valid
contrary evidence exists. July and August include only actual transactions.

## Evidence chain

Proposal → acceptance/contract → delivery/usage → legal invoice → payment
confirmation → bank/accounting reconciliation → P&L.

## P&L lines

Revenue:

- implementation;
- subscription;
- variable usage pass-through;
- separately scoped professional service.

COGS:

- Google Cloud gross usage;
- Vertex AI;
- WhatsApp/Meta;
- third-party APIs;
- direct onboarding/support labor.

Operating expenses:

- development labor;
- design;
- sales/marketing;
- accounting/legal;
- general software.

Credits are recorded separately from gross service usage. Templates live in
`product evidence/financial/`.
