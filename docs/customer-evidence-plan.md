# Customer evidence plan

For each pilot:

1. signed discovery/acceptance;
2. documented independence/relationship;
3. lawful data source and consent;
4. customer authorization for judge review;
5. tenant and user creation event;
6. timestamped product usage;
7. before/after metric;
8. proposal, contract or order;
9. valid invoice and payment proof when applicable;
10. testimonial with approved wording;
11. redacted public copy and private original hash.

Friendship alone is not treated as disqualifying, but IAPRO evidence should state
that Andrés and Rafael are friends and document the absence of common ownership,
control, historical service, or price manipulation.

Do not contact either customer until the staging flow, opt-out handling, and
approval controls have passed.
